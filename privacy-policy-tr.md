📌 Privacy Policy (100 Days: Island Survival)

Last Updated: December 2025

This mobile application (“100 Days: Island Survival”) respects your privacy. This document explains what data may be processed while using the app, including information handled by third-party services, and how we protect it.

🔐 1. Data Collected by the Developer

We do not collect, process, store, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as name, email, phone number, location, or contact details.

You can use the app completely anonymously.

Within the app, only the following data is stored locally on your device:

Game progress (e.g., collected items like water, food, wood, medicine, score, and day)

In-game achievements and scores

User settings and preferences (e.g., sound settings, language choice)

These data remain only on your device and are not uploaded or shared with the developer.
If the app is removed from the device, this data is automatically deleted.

📢 2. Push Notifications

The app may request permission to send notifications.
These notifications are only used for:

Gameplay reminders

In-game events or updates

You may disable notifications at any time in your device settings.
Apart from notification permission status, we do not collect or share any personal information.

📺 3. Third-Party Services (Advertising - Google AdMob)

The app displays ads using Google AdMob, which may collect certain technical and non-personal data in order to:

Provide personalized or non-personalized ads

Measure ad performance

Prevent fraud and misuse

AdMob may process (depending on consent and device settings):

Advertising ID (IDFA/AAID)

Approximate location (from IP address)

Device and platform information

These data are processed by Google, not by the app developer.

📌 Consent Requirement

For regions where privacy laws apply (e.g., GDPR in the EU, ATT on iOS), the app may request permission to allow personalized advertising. Personalized ads are shown only if the user agrees.

🔗 Google Ad Policies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

🍪 4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The app itself does not use cookies or tracking.
However, third-party services like Google AdMob may use cookies or similar technologies solely for advertising performance and personalization purposes.

🔐 5. Data Security

No personal data is collected by the developer.

No data is sent to any developer server or cloud storage.

Only third-party providers (e.g., AdMob) handle data under their own policies.

The app requires internet connection only for loading ads. The game works offline otherwise.

👶 6. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will assist if possible.
This app is designed for general audiences.
